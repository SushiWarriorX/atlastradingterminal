# Sources behind the btcquant strategy families

Written 2026-11-15, before any family was run.

**How to read this file.** The claims below are the authors' own, taken from abstracts, bibliographic records and secondary summaries seen on 2026-11-15. **The builder has not read the full text of any of them.** "Record confirmed" means the authors, title, journal, volume and pages were seen in a bibliographic database or a publisher or repository page on that day; it does not mean the claims were checked. Most of these studies are about other markets than Bitcoin; where one is about Bitcoin it says so. None of them is evidence that a rule will make money here. They are the reason a family is tested at all, and nothing more: every family is a hypothesis that the protocol (`../PROTOCOL.md`) tries to knock down. Several families have little or no published support, and the notes say so.

## What is not relied on

Blogs, trading-platform script pages, newsletters and forum posts that appeared in searches were not used for any rule, number or claim. Where a secondary summary is mentioned above it is named as one.

## Families with little or no published support

`rsi_momentum`, `bollinger_mr`, `rsi_oversold`, `vol_breakout` and `regime_switch` have practitioner folklore behind them and no peer-reviewed test on Bitcoin that was found. They are in the library because the mission lists them. The protocol's multiple-testing accounting counts all of their 48 configurations.
